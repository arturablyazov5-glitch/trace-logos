Reviews Exporter FAQ

How do I export reviews from Yandex Maps?

Open the business page on Yandex Maps and click the extension’s button in the interface: it scrolls the feed to the end by itself and saves the reviews with dates, ratings and reactions to .md. The only limit is how many reviews the platform serves.

How do I download reviews from Avito?

Open a seller’s reviews page or one of their listings on Avito and click the collect button. The seller’s name goes into the file header.

How do I export reviews from Ozon, and why can the export be incomplete?

Open the product page on Ozon and start collection. Ozon serves some reviews only to signed-in users — in that case the file header gets a note about an incomplete export. Sign in and run it again.

How do I export comments from Telegram?

Open the discussion thread under a channel post in the web version of Telegram and click the extension’s button. It goes up the thread to the first comment, then down to the end, marks attachments and adds the post’s text, date, views and reactions to the header.

Where do the collected reviews go?

Nowhere. The extension reads the page you already have open and builds the file right on your device. It has no server of its own and needs no sign-up.

Why .md and not Excel?

Markdown opens in any text editor, reads without conversion and fits entirely into an AI model’s context window when you need to boil reviews down to insights. A table is easy to build from it, too.

How do I install the extension, and which browsers does it work in?

It is not in the Chrome Web Store: download the archive, unzip it, turn on “Developer mode” at chrome://extensions and click “Load unpacked”. Any Chromium-based browser works: Chrome, Edge, Yandex Browser, Arc, Brave. Updates are installed manually, and the extension notifies you about a new version.

Is the extension free?

Yes, the price is up to you — you can download it for $0. More free tools are collected in the “Tools”.

Changelog

What changed in each version
v1.2.02026-08-07
  • Telegram Web support — collect comments on a channel post right from the open discussion thread, using the button in the interface
  • Two-phase thread autoscroll (first up to the first comment, then down to the end) — collects everything regardless of where Telegram opened the thread
  • Attachments are recognized: files/documents, voice messages and regular audio, round videos, photos and videos — each on its own line with an icon and, where available, size/duration
  • The document header includes the post’s text, date, views, forwards and reactions
  • Comments with no name, no text and no attachments (a bare photo/video without a caption) are left out of the final document
v1.1.12026-08-06
  • The reviews list now scrolls to the top automatically before collection starts if the page was already scrolled down
v1.1.02026-08-06
  • Avito support — collect a seller’s reviews from the reviews page or straight from a listing, using the button in the interface
  • The collection indicator was redesigned — a card with a progress bar and percentage in the center of the screen instead of highlighting the reviews block; the background is dimmed and blocked from clicks and scrolling during collection
  • The organization/seller name now appears in the exported file
v1.0.12026-08-06
  • Update notification — now shown right in the promo panel while reviews are being collected
v1.0.02026-08-06
  • First release: collect an organization’s reviews from Yandex Maps with autoscroll
  • Filtering of duplicates, empty reviews and emoji-only reviews
  • Likes/dislikes parsing, export to .md
