PDF → Figma FAQ

How do I open a PDF in Figma?

Run the plugin, drag a PDF into the panel or choose a file, then click “Convert”. Pages land as separate frames on the current Figma page, left to right. A password-protected PDF won’t open, so remove the protection first.

How do I import only some pages?

Enter page numbers and ranges in the pages field, for example “1-3, 7”; an empty field means all pages. If you first need to combine several documents, use the online tool “Merge PDF”.

What is the difference between Vector and Image mode?

“Vector” creates editable paths, fills and strokes. “Image” places the page as a PNG fill inside a frame, at 1× to 4× density, 2× by default. The frame size does not depend on density.

Can I edit PDF text in Figma?

Usually not: text is converted to curves when the embedded font data is available. Without it, you get regular text with a substituted font.

Will a scanned PDF become vector?

No. The plugin does not recognize text or trace scans, so raster inserts stay images. Ready-made vector brand logos are in the logo catalog.

Why does the page look different in Figma than in the PDF?

Vector import can differ from the original: soft masks become opaque, some fills are lost and hairlines look paler. For such a page, choose “Image”. If building the SVG fails, the plugin tries PNG on its own and says so in the final banner.

Is my PDF sent to a server?

No. Pages are processed locally inside the plugin, the engines are bundled into the panel and the manifest forbids network access.

Is the plugin free, and where does it work?

It is free; pay what you want. Download and unzip the archive, then in Figma Desktop choose Plugins → Development → Import plugin from manifest…. Figma Design and Figma Slides are supported; more plugins are collected in the “Tools”.
