Trace Typograf FAQ

How do I set non-breaking spaces in Taptop and Tilda?

Select a layer in Taptop or click a text field in Tilda, press the extension icon and “Apply typography”. Short words get tied to the next word, and numbers to units and currency.

Is this an extension or a Figma plugin?

It is a Chrome extension for website editors. For Figma layouts there is a separate Typograf plugin.

Where does the extension work?

Taptop, and fields on tilda.cc subdomains.

Does it keep my formatting?

Edits are made in place. Manual line breaks may get merged.

Why does nothing happen?

Reload the editor. Select a layer in Taptop or click a text field in Tilda.

Where does my text go?

Nowhere: text is processed on your device, in the browser.

Is it available in the Chrome Web Store?

Only manual installation in developer mode.

Is it free?

Yes, free, pay what you want. More tools for designers are in the Tools section.
