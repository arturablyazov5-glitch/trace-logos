Taptop Helper FAQ

How do I select multiple layers in Taptop?

Click layers while holding Cmd on Mac or Ctrl on Windows. Selected layers are deleted with Delete or Backspace.

How do I copy layers from one Taptop page to another?

Select the layers and press Cmd/Ctrl + C, go to another page and press Cmd/Ctrl + V. The clipboard is kept when you switch pages.

When is the clipboard cleared?

Reloading the page and Escape clear the clipboard. Switching pages does not.

How do I publish all pages in Taptop at once?

Press “Publish all” in the header of the Designer and Content modes: pages are published one by one. In Preview mode the button is hidden.

Can I close the tab while publishing?

No. Wait until publishing finishes in the open tab.

Is it free? Do I need a separate account?

Free, pay what you want. No separate account is needed: just your Taptop account and access to the project; the service plan stays as is.

Is there a typography tool for Taptop?

Yes, Trace Typograf sets non-breaking spaces, dashes and guillemets in text fields in Taptop and Tilda.

How do I install Taptop Helper?

Download the archive, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on developer mode and load the folder. Other free tools are in the Tools section.
