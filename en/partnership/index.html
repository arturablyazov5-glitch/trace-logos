Partnership

Free logos. A link in return.

Using our library on your website or in your service? Place the Trace Logos badge in your footer and link it to us so your users can find the logos they need too.

Get the badge Partnership terms

How it works

Download SVG and PNG files and add logos to mockups, presentations and interfaces. Trace Logos provides access for free, without registration or limits on the number of files.

Add the badge to your footer

Place it in a visible part of the footer of the website or service using our library. The badge must be clickable and link to Trace Logos. Keep its artwork and proportions.

A badge for your website

Enter your service name, copy the HTML and paste it into your footer. The badge link and code below update automatically.

For example, my-service. This becomes the utm_source value so we can see where a visitor came from.
