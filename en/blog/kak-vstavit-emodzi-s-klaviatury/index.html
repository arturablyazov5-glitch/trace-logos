How to Type Emoji from the Keyboard — Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android

On a phone, emoji are one key away — yet on a computer people still google "fire emoji" to copy it from search results. Meanwhile both Windows and macOS open an emoji panel with a single shortcut; it's just barely advertised. Here's every method worth knowing.

TL;DR

Windows: Win + period (.) opens the emoji panel. Mac: Ctrl + Cmd + Space (or the 🌐/fn key on recent keyboards). Both panels support search by name. That's the core — details and fallbacks below.

Windows: Win + period

Press Win and the period key. A panel opens with emoji, GIFs, kaomoji and symbols.

  • Search: start typing a name — "fire", "heart" — and the panel filters. Search language follows your keyboard layout.
  • Recents: the first tab remembers your frequent picks.
  • Works in any input field: browser, Word, Telegram Desktop, even a terminal commit message.

If the panel doesn't open, you're on something older than Windows 10 — fall back to the Character Map or copying from a catalog.

Mac: Ctrl + Cmd + Space

Control + Command + Space opens the "Emoji & Symbols" panel. MacBooks since 2020 also have a dedicated key: fn/🌐 in the bottom-left corner.

  • Search works by name.
  • Expanded mode: the button in the panel's top-right corner unfolds the full catalog with categories — including Unicode symbols, arrows and pictographs.
  • The panel can stay open above windows for inserting emoji in series.

iPhone and Android

Familiar territory with a few hidden tricks:

  • iPhone: long-press an emoji for skin-tone options. Typing a word ("heart") makes the predictive bar suggest the emoji — faster than browsing categories.
  • Android (Gboard): emoji search right on the keyboard, plus Emoji Kitchen — combining two emoji into a sticker.
  • Both platforms support dictation: say "smiley emoji" while voice-typing.

Emoji in documents and email

Word, Google Docs, Notion — the OS panels work anywhere a cursor blinks. Word quirk: emoji may render as black-and-white outlines — a font peculiarity; color returns on other devices.

Email. Emoji in subject lines display for most recipients, but old corporate Outlook builds may show boxes. Rule of thumb: marketing newsletters — fine and effective; first business contact with a stranger — skip them. More in emoji in marketing.

FAQ

Why do my emoji look different from my friend's? Every platform draws its own set: Apple, Google, Samsung differ. It's by design, not a bug — full story in why emoji look different everywhere.

How do I type an emoji missing from the panel? Panels show the Unicode set your OS knows. New emoji arrive with OS updates — update, or copy the character from a web catalog.

Are there text codes? Telegram, Slack and Discord support :fire:-style shortcodes. Plain input fields don't.

Why is the Win+period panel empty or not searching? Search follows the active keyboard language. An empty panel is a known glitch of old Windows 10 builds — usually fixed by updating.

Can emoji go into file and folder names? Yes — the OS panels work in the rename dialog, and both Windows and macOS file systems accept emoji. Think twice though: scripts, archivers and legacy software may trip over such names.

Can I bind my own shortcuts to specific emoji? Yes, via text replacement: iPhone/Mac — Text Replacement (e.g. "fff" → 🔥), Android — the Gboard dictionary, Windows — PowerToys. For your frequent emoji this beats any panel.

Emoji in professional tools

A few non-obvious places where emoji entry works differently:

  • Excel and Google Sheets: the OS panels work, and emoji can live inside formulas as text — e.g. visual status columns (=IF(B2>100,"🟢","🔴")). A legitimate way to make a report readable without conditional formatting.
  • Git commits: Win+period works in the terminal too, and gitmoji-convention projects encode change types with a leading emoji (✨ feature, 🐛 fix). Debatable but widespread.
  • Figma and mockups: OS shortcuts work in text layers, but you'll see your system's font. Where the emoji's exact look matters, place a platform-specific PNG (our catalog serves Apple and Google styles).
  • Passwords: many services technically accept emoji in passwords — but you can't type them on foreign devices and ATM keypads. Don't.

A bit of history: why computers "hid" emoji

A funny paradox: emoji were born on Japanese feature phones that had a dedicated key for them — while "grown-up" computers went a quarter century without a standard way to type them. The Win+period panel arrived only in 2017 (Windows 10 Fall Creators Update); the Mac symbols panel existed long before but hid in menus. The reason is simple: before smartphones, emoji were considered an SMS toy, not part of business writing. Today they're a full citizen of written speech, and this article's shortcuts deserve muscle memory: Win+period saves seconds dozens of times a day.

In short

Win+period and Ctrl+Cmd+Space cover 95% of cases. For the remaining 5% — a specific platform's artwork or high resolution — there's our emoji catalog: search in Russian or English, copy with a click, download Apple- or Google-style PNGs.
