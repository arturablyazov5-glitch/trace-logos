How to add the Apple Maps logo in Figma

Step 1 Open the Trace Logos plugin

In Figma: the Plugins menu → "Trace Logos". Install it once from the Figma Community if you haven't already.

Step 2 Search for "Apple Maps"

Type "Apple Maps" into the plugin's search field. The component is named "Icon / Map / AppleMaps" and includes every variant and style of the logo.

Step 3 Drop it on the canvas

Click the preview and the logo lands on your canvas as an editable vector layer, ready to recolor and export.
